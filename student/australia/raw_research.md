SECTION 1  —  Overview & Basic Eligibility Requirements

## 1.1  What Is the Subclass 500 Student Visa?
The Student visa (subclass 500) is Australia's primary visa for international students who want to study full-time at a CRICOS-registered institution for more than three months. It replaced the former Subclass 573/574/575 sector-specific visas under the Simplified Student Visa Framework (SSVF) introduced in 2016.

SECTION 2  —  Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE)

A CoE is issued by the registered education provider once a student has formally accepted an offer and paid any required fees. It is the mandatory gateway document for a subclass 500 application. Without a CoE the application is invalid.


SECTION 3  —  Genuine Student (GS) Requirement

⚠  MAJOR CHANGE from 23 March 2024: The old GTE requirement is replaced by the new Genuine Student (GS) requirement for all applications lodged on/after this date.

Under the GS requirement, the Department of Home Affairs assesses whether the applicant is genuinely intending to study in Australia. Unlike the old GTE, the GS framework explicitly acknowledges that post-study permanent residence pathways exist and do not automatically count against an applicant.
